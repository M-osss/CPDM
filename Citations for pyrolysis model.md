Citations for pyrolysis model

reactions taken from [1] Spilimbergo, A. P., Krioukov, V. G., & Abdulline, A. L. (n.d.). Pyrolysis of ethane: Equilibrium and kinetic models and the reduction of reaction mechanism. Chemical Engineering Journal, 120(3), 249-257. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2006.03.008 

some basic formulations are taken from [2] Ibrahim, H. G., Alagta, A. R., & Emhamed, A. M. (2015). Optimal Design of Ethane Pyrolysis Reactor. International Journal of Science and Research (IJSR), 4(1), 1978-1983. https://www.ijsr.net/archive/v4i1/NOV142876.pdf

additional modifications were made to reactions mechanisms from [1] to account for coke deposition on the walls as well as transient heat transfer, i.e. heating curve and 3D CFD modelling. That is, chemistry stayed approximately the same but more robust treatment of transport phenomena was applied.
